Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Customer Service take to reply?

We reply on WhatsApp in under 10 minutes during working hours, and within 24 hours for tickets filed here.

I paid a seller but did not receive my product. What do I do?

File a complaint with category "Order Issue", include the seller name, amount paid, and proof of payment. Admin will review it and contact the seller.

How do I report a scam seller?

Choose "Report Seller", add the seller name and screenshots. Admin will investigate and can remove the seller from the Marketplace.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled between you and the seller first. If the seller refuses, Admin will step in after reviewing your complaint and proof.
